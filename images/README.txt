IMAGES: what goes here

  profile.jpg            Your photo. Square, about 600x600, under 100 KB. (TODO(you))
  projects/NAME.webp     One screenshot per project. 1280x800, under 120 KB, WebP.
  og-image.png           The preview card shown when you share your link. Already made (1200x630).
                         Re-make it if you change your name or role.
  favicon.svg            Browser tab icon (your initials).
  apple-touch-icon.png   Home-screen icon on iPhone.

Free tools: squoosh.app converts and shrinks images in the browser.
Always write real alt text, for example: "Screenshot of the Movie Search app showing results for 'Inception'".
